# Third-party components and services

HyperHex uses third-party software and external resources. Naresh Yegireddi's
copyright notice for original HyperHex contributions does not replace the rights,
licenses, or attribution requirements of those materials.

**Inventory status:** this document identifies principal components and services
visible in the source. It is not an exhaustive software bill of materials, a completed
license audit, or a replacement for required upstream license and notice text.
Before redistributing an artifact, review the exact included versions and retain
the notices required by their licenses.

## Geographic imagery and attribution

The globe uses OpenStreetMap's standard tile service. OpenStreetMap data is subject
to the Open Database License and requires attribution; the tile service has separate
usage requirements. These obligations are not replaced by HyperHex's code license.

Keep the required OpenStreetMap credit visible in the map, including on mobile and
when overlays are open. This inventory does not assert that every current layout
has been visually checked. Consult the provider's policies before scaling usage,
caching, or planning offline distribution:

- [OpenStreetMap attribution guidelines](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Attribution_Guidelines)
- [OpenStreetMap tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/)

Drone route requests and closures in the demonstration are generated by HyperHex.
They are not records of real drone movements, official corridor designations, or
restrictions supplied by OpenStreetMap.

## Fonts, assets, and externally loaded resources

The root document requests JetBrains Mono and Space Grotesk through Google Fonts.
Review the specific font licenses and retain required notices if bundling fonts for
self-hosting or redistribution.

Logo descriptors reference platform-managed asset paths. Verify the origin, rights,
and availability of each asset before repackaging it. This document does not certify
asset provenance or establish a separate license for the HyperHex name or logo.

CesiumJS scripts, styles, and related resources loaded outside the package manager
must be included in a release's dependency review. Preserve their required credits
and notices rather than treating the package lockfile as a complete inventory.

## Release review

For a redistributable or archived release, record exact dependencies, incorporate
required upstream license/notice text, review copied source and assets, and check
runtime services separately from bundled software. Alternative commercial terms
for HyperHex do not automatically grant new rights in any third-party component.

See [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) for HyperHex's licensing summary and
[development and deployment](docs/development.md) for the current external-resource
integrations.
